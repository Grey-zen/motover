Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

At MotoTabi, we take the protection and responsible handling of your personal information seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use our website.

Privacy & Data

Personal Information Policy

We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide a useful and reliable website experience.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through contact forms, newsletter subscriptions, or other communication features available on the website.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

2. How We Use Information

Information provided to MotoTabi may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests.
  • Improve website content and user experience.
  • Provide important website or service updates.
  • Deliver newsletter communications when requested.

3. Information Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should understand that internet communication always involves some level of risk.

4. Cookies & Analytics

MotoTabi may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand how visitors interact with the website, and improve overall site performance.

Cookies may also be used by third-party analytics or advertising services where applicable.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

MotoTabi may use third-party services such as analytics, hosting, content delivery, advertising, or other technology providers.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

6. Information Sharing

MotoTabi does not intentionally sell or unlawfully disclose personal information to third parties.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to operate the website, provide requested services, comply with applicable law, protect website security, or respond to lawful requests.

7. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. MotoTabi is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of websites operated by third parties.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website before providing personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

MotoTabi does not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal information from children through the website.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, technology, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page. We recommend checking this page periodically for the latest information.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, how information is handled, or your interactions with MotoTabi, please contact us through our Contact page .
